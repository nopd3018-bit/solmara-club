About us

×Solmara Club is a Thailand-based active lifestyle brand and community that brings people together through sports, fitness and social experiences. Most run clubs only run. We do that too, but we also train, head outdoors, make things in creative workshops and throw community events. It is a space to stay active, meet like-minded people, find new interests and build friendships that go beyond sport.
